WHAT THE OWNER'S NOTION RESTS ON (read this first)
Reza answered "Is this a food photo?" for 35 public photos: 25 DISPUTED (the judge said yes, the blind labellers said
no_match or unsure) and 10 clear-food controls. 25 answers, one person, and the 25 were SELECTED as disagreements, not
drawn uniformly. Yes-rate by failure kind (eval/vector_enough/false_positives_scene.txt kinds; 95% Clopper-Pearson):

  F5 edible thing not served as food   1/2   [0.01-0.99]     F6 produce display                 1/1  [0.03-1.00]
  F7 food event, no food visible       1/1   [0.03-1.00]     controls (labellers: match)        9/10 [0.55-1.00]
F3, F6, F7 rest on ONE answer each, F4 and F5 on two. The one control he rejected: a heap of string-tied steamed hairy
crabs in front of diners (viewed) - his notion is not simply "broader" everywhere.

METHOD (task 1)
Every labelled food photo TAGGED with a kind whose label is not a match counts as w of a match, w = his yes-rate for
that kind (point); matches stay 1; untagged labels keep their label. Each table is re-scored exactly as it was made
(same labels V1 / V2 / V3, same cut configuration for the census screens, same systems scored jointly, same seed;
eval_recall.score_systems gained an optional weight= that is a no-op when absent). The labellers' column reproduces every
published number (R45 .81/.80/.57, R46 .60/.68/.78/.87/.94, R47 .70/.80/.94/.86/.75/.88). Sensitivities:
  CP ends    every kind at its 95% CP low end / high end at once.
  + my tags  I viewed and tagged (same kinds) the 22 untagged not-a-match audit4 labels with P >= 0.99 (9 of them in
             the shipped kept set: 5 F1, 2 F5, 1 F2, 1 not food) - they are mostly V3 extras, so this moves R47 only.
  + rejected I also viewed and tagged 48 food-adjacent not-a-match labels of photos the judge REJECTS (P < 0.99):
             meals with people, drinks as the subject, pets eating, catches. He was never asked about such photos.

READING
- Precision: by his notion food search is NOT half wrong. The shipped rule is .80 by the labellers and .89 by him (.94
  with my tags); the old 0.99 fast-mode answer that started this (R45: .57) is .84 by him. Most of the "error" was the
  labellers' definition (meals with people, drinks), not the judge seeing food that is not there. What he rejects is
  the shop front with no food (0/7).
- Recall: the strict cuts cost HIM more than they cost the labellers, because what 0.9999 removes is mostly photos he
  calls food. Directly from his answers: of the disputed photos the shipped rule keeps he says yes to 10/13; of the
  disputed photos it removes (P in [0.99, 0.9999)) 6/12 (in [0.999, 0.9999): 3 people-at-a-meal/pet yes, 3 shop fronts
  no). Shipped-rule recall .947 (labellers) -> .829 (his, tags only) -> .784 (+ my tags).
- So the R47 food cut 0.9999 wins only by the labellers' notion; by his it ties 0.99 / 0.999 (tags only) or loses to
  them (+ my tags). The "main subject" wordings (g_main, f_meal, the people veto) are worst for him (recall .55-.73).
- His recall is an UPPER BOUND. He was asked only about photos the judge accepted. If judge-rejected meals-with-people /
  drinks / pets-eating photos are food to him at the same rates (my 48 tags; each B-stratum label stands for ~5
  photos), his total rises 149 -> 198 and shipped recall falls to .589. Not counted even there: 5 party / kitchen photos
  with incidental drinks in the bulk stratum (each label ~37 photos) that I tagged "none"; if he counted those, ~185 more.
  The precision column is the robust part of this section; the recall column is not.

(2) SHOP-FRONT VETO (task 2): keep = shipped food rule AND veto P >= cut
Wordings (same phone-weight pipeline as eval/eval_scene_questions.py gen; P = P(yes); keep when P >= cut):
  v_real "Is real food or a drink visible in this photo (not just a sign, menu or shop front)?"
  v_any  "Can you see any actual food or drink in this photo, even a small amount, rather than only a restaurant, shop
          front, sign, menu or food van?"
Judged: 164 audit4 photos with q0 P >= 0.99 (a superset of the 125 the shipped rule keeps) + the 50 RESULTS 35 (2)
other-library food photos; plus "Is this a photo of food?" re-judged in the same job: keep at 0.9999 identical for
163/164, max |dP| 0.0026. Cut chosen on one half (item_id parity), applied to the other, both ways; objective on the
choosing half = owner's (wrong removed - real removed), labelled shipped photos of both sets; candidates no veto, 0.01 ..
0.99. Chosen: v_real 0.01 on both halves; v_any 0.01 on one half, no veto on the other (unstable).
Every shop front in the shipped set (6: 4 audit4, 2 other) gets veto P < 0.5 from both wordings (v_real < 0.01 for 5/6;
the hot-dog/popcorn van 0.32).

(3) RECOMMENDATION: do NOT ship the veto now.
- Its effect on the shipped rule is 4 wrong photos removed of 125 for 1 real one: precision +.025 (labellers) / +.029
  (owner), recall -.009 / -.007, inside the intervals. The 0.9999 cut already removed 5 of the 8 tagged shop fronts.
- It costs one more judge call for every photo the food rule keeps (125 here, +26% on a 480-call first answer; the
  phone cost with image-prefix reuse is unmeasured).
- The wording is fragile: a near-paraphrase (v_any) throws away packaged food and dried fish that the owner calls food
  (he said yes to the Lay's bag and the Cheerios box), and v_real itself drops a shelf of chip bags.
- The bigger lever for the owner is the CUT, not the veto: by his notion the 0.9999 food cut costs recall (.83 vs .89 at
  0.999, tags only) for precision he mostly does not need. The one combination that is better for him -
  q0 >= 0.999 & v_real >= 0.01 (owner + my tags .940 / .857 vs shipped .935 / .784) - is worse by the labellers'
  notion (.735 vs .800 precision) and was found after looking, so it is a candidate, not a decision.
- Decisive next step (cheap, on the Mac sheet): ask him the 13 still-unanswered photos of the [0.999, 0.9999) band (the
  19 photos the 0.9999 cut removes; 6 answered: 3 yes, 3 shop fronts no). If he says yes to most non-shop-front ones,
  switch food to 0.999 + v_real >= 0.01; if not, keep 0.9999 and no veto.

CAVEATS
- The owner's notion = 25 selected disputed answers + 10 controls from one person, asked out of a search context.
- Kind rates are conditional on the judge saying yes; applying them to judge-rejected photos (+ rejected column) is an
  assumption, shown only to size the recall caveat.
- Tags beyond false_positives_scene.txt are mine (viewed at native pixels); they are in the two my_kind_tags files and
  used only in the columns marked "+ my tags" / "+ rejected" (the main owner column follows the task rule).
- One public set (4 Flickr libraries, ~500 px); the veto cut 0.01 was chosen on ~145 photos with ~6 shop fronts.
