LONG VIDEOS (>= 80 s): does the phone's sweep 2 find more than sweep 1's 3 frames?   (10-12; eval/eval_video_long.py)

QUESTION. Sweep 1 indexes 3 frames per video (cover/start + middle + end); sweep 2 a 4-s grid. RESULTS 37/37b measured
522 Pexels clips, none >= 80 s: no gain (216 vs 219 of 303, +13/-16, p = 0.71). Reza has 2,143 videos >= 80 s.

NOTE ON WHAT SHIPS. Sweep 2 is NOT "every 4 s" for long videos: videoSampleTimes(everySeconds: 4, maxFrames: 40)
(FindPicsCore LazyVideo.swift) caps it at 40 evenly spaced frames, so above 156 s the spacing is duration/39
(10 min -> one frame per ~15 s). Both the shipped (capped) rule and an uncapped 4-s grid are measured below.

DATASET. Wikimedia Commons videos (free licences: CC BY / BY-SA 2.0-4.0, CC0, PD), harvested by search on 78 everyday
words (walk, dog, beach, birthday, train ride, fireworks, market, ...), filtered 80-900 s, short side >= 480 px,
recorded 2000 or later, title blocklist (lecture, news, trailer, ...), <= 3 per uploader, stratified by length
(330 candidates). Eye screen of every candidate's poster (eval/video_sampling/long/poster_sheets): 147 rejected as not
personal-video-like (TV news, speeches/lectures, produced shows, animation, screencasts, CCTV/bodycam, archival).
183 downloaded (1080p transcode 125, original 51, 480p 2; 25.9 GB); 5 dropped (2 decode errors, 2 unreadable
stream duration, 1 decoder stopped at 339 of 529 s; eval/video_sampling/long/dropped.txt).
FINAL: 178 videos, 131 uploaders, 13.2 h, 47,646 frames on the 1-s grid.
  length: 80-180 s 73 (median 119 s) | 3-6 min 63 (median 252 s) | 6-10 min 27 (median 420 s) | 10-14.5 min 15 (median 708 s)
  quantiles: min 81 s, p25 125 s, median 207 s, p75 334 s, p90 531 s, max 868 s.
  Content: walks, rides (train, tram, bike, motorbike, car), festivals/parades/markets, dances, fireworks, animals,
  nature, drone flights; personal-like but NOT iPhone footage (some drone/GoPro, some lightly edited).

HARNESS (same as RESULTS 37; eval_video_sampling._decode / picks reused unchanged):
  - 1-s grid, first decoded frame at/after each second, <= 1280 px; every rule's target times snap to grid seconds.
  - 40 queries (seed 0): a RANDOM grid frame of a random video -> 9B describes it -> 9B writes a search query (same
    prompts as eval_video.gen) -> product planner (plan_turn) -> judge question + looks.
  - Oracle: Qwen3.5-9B judge on ALL 47,646 grid frames per query (1.9 M judge calls, 16 GPU shards).
    Truth STRICT = >= 2 grid frames judged yes (p >= 0.7): 184 (query, video) pairs; lenient (>= 1 frame): 243.
  - Search as the phone does it: per video, the rule's frame with the best text score (PE-Core-B-16 = the phone's
    model; PE-Core-L-14-336 = section 37's model, as a check) is judged ONCE; found = p >= 0.7.
  - Paired two-sided sign test on the same (query, video) truth pairs.

Robustness checks
  - Not one or two queries: sweep 2 > sweep 1 on 24 of 40 queries, < on 1, = on 15. Gains spread over 25 queries
    (most: q2 "crowd in colourful clothes, tree branches in foreground" +9, q38 "forest path" +5) and 37 videos.
  - Seed videos (the video the query was written from, at a random moment) are 14 of the 42 gains: they are exactly
    the "the moment is somewhere in the middle of a long video" case. Without them: sweep 1 29/145, sweep 2 50/145.
  - Cheaper rules vs sweep 2 shipped (B-16), all not significant: every 8 s +3-5 / +6-9 / +6-9 by bucket;
    sweep 1 + 1 per 30 s +3-8 / +7-11 / +6-9. Their point estimates are lower; the 40-frame cap does not hurt
    (capped 81 vs uncapped 74 with B-16, 74 vs 82 with L-14: noise either way).
  - 80-180 s: B-16 +9-2 (p = 0.07), L-14 +6-7 (p = 1.0): no established gain, consistent with Pexels < 80 s.
  - Absolute level is low for long videos: even judging the best of ALL 1-s frames finds 75/184 (B-16). With one
    judged frame per video, the text ranking picking the wrong moment is the larger loss in long videos (not measured
    here: judging top-k frames per long video).

  Contrast with RESULTS 21 (short Pexels clips): there 5 of 8 extra-frame gains were judge false positives. Here most
  gains are a different MOMENT of the video, which sweep 1's 3 frames never see.

RECOMMENDATION: keep sweep 2, only for videos >= 180 s (drop it below 3 min).
  - >= 3 min: sweep 2 roughly doubles what sweep 1 finds (3-6 min 15 -> 29 of 73; 6+ min 6 -> 20 of 60), p < 0.01 with
    both ranking models, gains mostly real by eye (7/10).
  - 80-180 s: not established (+9-2 p = 0.07 with the phone's model, +6-7 with L-14); < 80 s: no gain (RESULTS 37b).
    Cost saved by skipping it there: ~28 frames per 80-180 s video.
  - Keep the 40-frame cap (no measured loss). A cheaper long-video rule (every 8 s, or sweep 1 + one frame per 30 s:
    ~19 frames for 6+ min) is not significantly worse but its point estimates are lower; not worth a change now.
  - Total found with "sweep 1 < 180 s, sweep 2 >= 180 s": 74/184 vs 81/184 for sweep 2 everywhere (difference = the
    80-180 s bucket, +2 -9 there) at 24.8 vs 36.1 frames per video on this length mix.

CAVEATS: truth is the 9B judge (1 FP in 10 gains looked at); 40 queries, truth pairs clustered (q2 32, q38 21 of 184);
Commons footage is a stand-in for iPhone video (1080p transcodes, some drone/GoPro); one search per video judged once.
