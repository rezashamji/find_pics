    sunset photos sit between P 0.99 and 0.999. With unsure = no match (the RESULTS 34 / 45 convention) there is no flag.
  - The completeness bound gets a little weaker. 'At most N missed' is driven by the 150-photo random sample (0 hits),
    so it stays at 273 to 307 while the found count shrinks. The recall lower bound shown (relative to judge-yes) goes from
    beach .503 -> .499, sunset .383 -> .347, food .354 -> .329.
  - Only 3 subject searches have stored P plus eye labels. ev16 (the 16-library validation of RESULTS 41/43/44) cannot be
    replayed: it stores only yes sets at a uniform 0.7 cut, with no P. So ev16's subject searches were never replayed at
    0.99 either.
  - The 'head rule fires earlier' risk is real in principle: fewer yeses can only make the window rule fire sooner.
    Here it didn't, because at 0.99 the last 100 head photos already held < 3 yes exactly where the rule fired, and
    one check (50 photos) earlier they held >= 3 yes at 0.999 too. A subject whose yeses sit mostly in
    P [0.99, 0.999) (e.g. church, -6 of 28 right photos in RESULTS 35 (2)) could stop one or more chunks earlier;
    this is unmeasured.
  - Estimator noise: sunset fast R (.931) > exhaustive R (.925) at 0.99, although the fast set is a subset. Each
    returned set gets its own stratum-cell match rates, so a difference of ~0.01 is noise.
  - 44b keep-looking continuation (assumed m4 hot-floor pace): the recall gap stays the same size up to 90 min
    (sunset .929 -> .914, beach .963 -> .957); precision keeps the gain (sunset .67 -> .82, food .61 -> .68).
