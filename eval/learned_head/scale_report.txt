M48-SCALE: DOES THE LEARNED HEAD GENERALISE TO UNSEEN QUERIES WITH ~10x MORE QUESTIONS? (2026-10-12; public data only)
Follows eval/learned_head/report.txt (M48). Code: eval/learned_head/{pack,gen_questions,select_pairs,label,train_scale,
scale_analyze,blend,rank_replay,scale_sheets}.py. Outputs: eval/learned_head/scale/ (curve.txt, curve_blend.txt,
tables_<run>.txt, rank_replay.txt, rank_replay_blend.txt, filter_report.txt, parity.txt, sheets/*.jpg).

SHORT ANSWER
1. Head ALONE, unseen everyday concepts on unseen libraries: NO. With 10,000 generated questions it is still BELOW the
   phone's cosine: EV16 AP .617 vs .692 (11 searches), A4 AP .709 vs .748, A4 eye precision / recall at the judge's own
   count .788 / .766 vs cosine .814 / .791. The curve rises only slowly (EV16 AP .595 -> .602 -> .617 at 2.4k / 5k /
   10k questions, ~+0.011 per doubling; extrapolated, cosine's .692 needs ~7 more doublings, ~10^5-10^6 questions).
   Mechanism (by search): it loses on the OBJECT searches whose whole semantic neighbourhood was excluded from training
   (boat AP .78 -> .21, dog .47 -> .25, car .62 -> .42, bicycle .80 -> .68) and wins on scenes / facts (church .59 ->
   .79, night .75 -> .87, beach .78 -> .82). 71k training photos full of cars, boats and pets were "no" for every one of
   11k questions, so the head learned "boat-like photo -> low P" as a prior; an unseen boat query cannot undo it.
2. Head alone, unseen PLANNER questions whose neighbourhood IS covered (the 2,412 real judge_distill questions, never in
   training): YES, it ranks better than cosine, and more questions help: AUC among each question's 24 most similar photos
   .716 -> .728 -> .736 vs cosine .634 (1,658 questions with both classes); .730 vs .679 even when the nearest training
   question is < 0.80 text cosine. Held-out generated questions: AUC .775 -> .796 -> .821 vs .621, AP .622 -> .647 -> .681
   vs .503.
3. BLEND = z(cosine) + z(logit head), equal weights fixed before looking (no tuning): better than cosine on EVERY test,
   including the adversarial everyday set, at every curve point. N10000 blend: EV16 AP .724 vs .692; A4 AP .792 vs .748;
   A4 eye precision / recall at the judge's count .847 / .825 vs cosine .814 / .793 (judge .933 / .909). The number of
   questions barely matters for the blend (eye .838 -> .836 -> .847); the 2.4k real-planner set (JDonly blend) is as
   good (.848 / .822, EV16 AP .763).
4. As fast mode's RANKING (same judge, shipped stop rule, 20 seeds): blend reaches cosine's first-answer found count
   with 23% fewer judge calls on audit4 (2,657 vs 3,453) and 22% fewer on ev16 without selfies (54,907 vs 70,687; with
   selfies 28%). JDonly blend: 33% / 35%. Head alone: 27% / 25% MORE calls (worse).
5. Verifier: no, for any kind on unseen queries. Best kind (night, a 'fact' search): precision at 80% of the judge's
   recall .74 (judge = 1); scenes .46-.62. The head is a ranker.
GO / NO-GO: ship the BLEND as fast mode's ranking (go, pending phone measurements below); do NOT ship the head alone as a
ranker, and NOT as a verifier for any kind. The 10x question data is not what makes it work; the cheapest shippable
version is the 2.4k-question head (JDonly, 8.9 MB fp16) blended with the existing cosine.

RECOMMENDATION
1. Ranker: adopt cosine + head blend for fast mode's order (object/scene/fact searches; not person-in-face-set searches,
   untested). Use the JDonly head (2.4k real planner questions; best numbers, smallest data); 8.9 MB fp16, one 4096->1024
   ->256->1 MLP per photo per search (~4.7 MFLOP after folding the text, report.txt budget). Before shipping: Swift port
   with a golden-vector parity test, on-device time for a 187k library (estimate 0.1-2 s), and a replay of the blend on
   the RESULTS 47 rule with the new beach/food questions.
2. Do not use the head alone: on concepts whose neighbourhood is absent from training it is worse than cosine (prior
   suppression), and nobody can guarantee coverage of what people type.
3. Do not spend more GPU on question count: 2.4k -> 10k moved blend eye precision .838 -> .847 and head-alone EV16 AP
   .595 -> .617. If more data is ever generated, generate it for coverage of concept families (vehicles, animals,
   food...) with full-library negatives, and keep scenes in "Is this a photo of X?" form; that is what E4 (seen concept,
   eye .88) and JDonly (neighbours seen) point to.
4. Verifier: no kind qualifies on unseen queries; the judge stays the only verifier (as report.txt).
