COMPLEX-QUERY BENCHMARK (10-12) - eval/complex_bench/
Public data only: the 57 DISBench Flickr users' own libraries (109,467 photos, native 500 px, dates 100%, GPS 98%).

WHAT WAS BUILT
1. Facts (facts.py): Qwen3.5-27B-FP8 (vLLM) wrote a structured description of every photo: caption, image type
   (selfie/screenshot/document/photo of a screen/print/artwork...), scene, up to 30 objects, live animals vs
   depictions, people count + up to 3 people (age, clothing colours, activity), activities, visible text, colours +
   notable colour, lighting, weather, quality (blurry/dark/...), style (b&w, close-up, long exposure...), occasion,
   landmark. 109,284 / 109,467 photos parsed (183 malformed JSON). facts/<user>.jsonl (92 MB).
   Metadata per library (prep.py -> libraries/): 3-hour-gap events, place names from GPS addresses, face groups
   (product face model); the two largest face groups (>= 25 photos) are named "Sam" and "Alex" (the product's
   "name a face group" path), present in the libraries that have them.
2. Requests + truth programs (gen.py): the same 27B (text, thinking on) read a digest of each library (days with
   places and most frequent things, concept counts, looks, distinctive photos with ids, people) and wrote owner-style
   requests in 8 categories plus a TRUTH PROGRAM for each: yes/no questions about one photo + exact metadata
   (dates, hours, place words, days, anchor photo ids + window, named face group). 1,873 requests for 57 libraries.
   "Today" per library = last photo + 75 days, so "last summer"/"two years ago" resolve.
3. Oracle (oracle.py): the 27B answered EVERY program question on EVERY photo of the library (P(yes) from next-token
   probabilities, the product judge's prompt format); person questions on the person's photos with the red box.
   4.35 M (photo, question) answers so far, 2,309 distinct questions, 45 libraries (oracle/<user>.parquet).
4. Candidate answer sets (build.py): program + oracle -> sets; requests dropped when empty, > 25% of the library,
   two-album overlap > 20%, an anchor description that also fits photos in other windows (ambiguous), or a
   half-remembered photo whose description fits > 6 photos. Fixes decided from the audit (below): unstated
   time/place dropped for d/h only; unstated visual filters dropped in "photos from <moment>" requests (d);
   per-category oracle cut a=0.7 b=0.8 c=0.5 d=0.8 e=0.5 f=0.9 g=0.5 h=0.9.

    g recall 0.50: two-album programs over-narrow ("roller derby April 2012 vs flag football July 11 2012": the
      flag-football album has 1 photo, the eye finds many).

  requests raised d recall 0.72 -> 0.93 at the same precision.

MOST COMMON FAILURE KINDS (random 40 of the 101 dev requests with F1 < 0.5, read plan vs truth):
  1. Time grounding, 6/40: unstated year resolved to the future or wrong occurrence ("the concert on November 27th",
     today 2014-07-08 -> 2014-11-27; "the day we hiked in Denali two years ago" -> exactly 2010-12-25); the event
     dropped from the plan ("photos from the wedding three years ago" -> all 1,230 photos of 2011).
  2. Moments become windows that are too wide, or per-photo questions, 7/40: "the round lake hike two years ago" ->
     anchor "Round Lake" + same_event = 154 photos (truth 16); "the rainy hike in March" -> "Is there rain in this
     photo?" = 22 of 107; "when we were renovating the room in 2011" -> same_year.
  3. Follow-up edits, 6/40: "add the ones with the singer too" compiled as AND, not OR (0 returned); "actually show me
     all the sea lion photos from that day" keeps the earlier narrowing; "I want the train ones, not the rickshaws"
     keeps "street scene" and never adds trains.
  4. The 9B judge stricter than the 27B on looks, 8/40 (part truth over-inclusion, see c): "reflections in the
     water" 15 vs 83; "golden sunlight" 93 vs 393; "overexposed photos of the mountains" filtered out 963.
  5. Anchor never found, 3/40: compound descriptions ("the woman hugging the inflatable Iron Man doll", "the bike
     covered in Christmas lights") -> 0 anchor photos -> nothing returned; same_place = exact address string (4
     photos vs the truth's 25 for the city).
  6. Place names matched literally, 3/40: "New York City" vs addresses saying "New York County"; "Croatia" vs local-
     language addresses; hockey in "Los Angeles" vs "Anaheim".
  7. Over-inclusion on named people, 4/40: "Sam wearing the blue surgical cap from when she was in the hospital" -> 51
     photos (truth 3), the hospital moment ignored.
  8. Metric artefact, 1/40: a half-remembered photo found among 8 returned scores F1 0.22 (use the f hit rate).

WHAT TRAINING DATA THIS ENABLES (train split only; test never; dev for selection)
  - Planner: (request [+ conversation], library context) -> truth program pairs, 320 train requests now (~700 when the
    remaining 12 train libraries finish), covering anchors/windows, two albums, OR/undo edits, relative dates against a
    per-library today; failure kinds 1-3 and 5-6 above are exactly what such pairs teach.
  - Judge / learned scorer: 4.35 M (photo, question) 27B probabilities on full libraries (2,309 questions), plus 2,336
    eye labels (audit/, audit2/) for calibration; the 27B cut sweep (audit/cut_sweep.txt) shows where it is wrong.
  - Dense per-photo facts for all 109,467 photos: captions/tags for retrieval or distillation of a phone tagger.
  - End-to-end set targets per request for a learned head over stored embeddings (M48), with categories marked
    verified/provisional.

    programs or eye-label their answer sets).
