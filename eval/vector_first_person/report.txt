M45 (MAC_INBOX): does RESULTS 45's vector-first rule hold INSIDE a face-filtered set? (10-12; public data only)
Code: eval/eval_vector_first_person.py (gen / imdb / analyze / sheets). Full tables: report_auto.txt (same folder).

QUESTION
The phone shows a vector-ranked first screen with no judge call when the 20th-best PE-Core-B-16 look score in scope is
>= 0.2074 (firstScreenDecision), and per photo at >= 0.2036 (likelyAtOnce). Person searches are excluded
(vectorFirstIneligible) only because RESULTS 45 never measured them. Here: rank ONE person's photos (the product's face
filter) by the look vector, apply the same tests, compare with the judge and with eye labels.

  face filter (99 / 74 / 66). Labels: era (heavy vs lean, from published weight losses; a proxy, not a per-photo look);

RESULTS (denominators = photos on screens, pooled over the 32 people; judge-rel = share the judge says yes to)

1. The shipped rule NEVER fires inside a person set: s20 >= 0.2074 in 0/256 searches (max 0.2063, sunglasses).
   So applied as-is, person+look would always go to the judge: safe, and useless.
2. Scores run lower inside a person set, as suspected. s20 median 0.154 (p10 0.123, p90 0.186) vs 0.174 (0.140, 0.212)
   on RESULTS 45's 216 whole-library searches; among 'enough' searches 0.169 vs 0.2025. s1 median 0.198 vs 0.220.
3. A lower threshold does NOT rescue it. 'Enough' (>= 18 of the vector top 20 judge-yes) is 42/256. s20 separates it
   only weakly (AUC 0.72). Even the highest bin, s20 in [0.19, 0.2074), is enough in 9/20 searches; s20 >= 0.18: 15/42;
   >= 0.17: 19/65. The 'safe' refit (lowest T with >= 90% enough among >= 5 training searches) finds NO threshold in
   17/17 leave-one-library-out folds, nor on all 256. There is no T for person searches.
4. First-screen precision, vector-only vs judge (judge-rel.; "judge's screen" = its own top 20 by P):
     smiling    vector 438/640 = 0.68 | judge 584/640       outdoors   vector 542/640 = 0.85 | judge 605/640
     sunglasses vector 271/640 = 0.42 | judge 350/640       hat        vector 247/640 = 0.39 | judge 342/640
     beach      vector 170/640 = 0.27 | judge 224/640       eating     vector  82/640 = 0.13 | judge  97/640
     snow       vector  37/640 = 0.06 | judge  52/640       tired      vector 124/640 = 0.19 | judge 369/640
     ALL        vector 1911/5120 = 0.37 [0.36-0.39]; at 50: 3915/12800 = 0.31 vs judge 4801/12800 = 0.38.
   Most of the gap is base rate, not ranking: a person's ~100 photos hold a median of 4 beach / 0 snow / 2 eating /
   10 hat photos, so no screen of 20 can be right. Within a person the vector RANKS well for scene/object looks (median
   AUC vs judge: snow 0.99, eating 0.93, beach 0.89, outdoors 0.89, hat 0.86, sunglasses 0.83) and badly for
   expressions/states (smiling 0.68, tired 0.61). Where a full right screen exists (>= 20 judge-yes; 98/256 searches)
   the vector top 20 is 1520/1960 = 0.78 judge-rel. (RESULTS 45's 'vectors' searches: 0.958).
5. EYE (208 blind labels; unsure = not a match). Eye rate of judge-yes photos in the vector top 20: outdoors 10/10,
   sunglasses 10/10, smiling 9/10, hat 9/10, snow 6/10 (4 unsure), beach 5/10 (3 unsure), eating 5/10 (3 unsure),
   tired 0/10 (6 unsure). Of judge-no photos in the vector top 20: 6/80 right by eye (smiling 2, outdoors 2, hat 1,
   beach 1). Estimated eye precision of the screen:
     vector top 20 ~0.34 (~1,724 of 5,120 photos right)  vs  the judge's screen ~0.76 (~1,992 of 2,623 photos right).
   The judge's screen is shorter (it shows only yes photos) and right more often; by condition the judge's screen is
   0.95-1.00 for outdoors / sunglasses / hat / smiling and the vector screen 0.41-0.88.
   "TIRED" FAILS FOR BOTH INSTRUMENTS: 0 of 16 judge-yes photos were clearly tired by eye (9 unsure). The judge's 'yes'
   to "Does the person in the red box look tired?" is not a usable signal on these photos either.
6. Per photo (likelyAtOnce >= 0.2036 inside person sets): 523 of 5,120 top-20 slots shown, judge-rel. 401/523 = 0.77
   [0.73-0.80] vs RESULTS 45's 0.89. Fine for worn things / scenes (sunglasses 114/129, beach 100/114, hat 85/101,
   smiling 30/34 = 0.84-0.88) but wrong for states: eating 32/61 = 0.52, tired 18/55 = 0.33.
7. IMDB-WIKI eras (truth = era, a proxy; 239 face-filtered crops). Vector top 20 right era: 'heavier' 15/20, 15/20,
   13/20 (Pratt, Hill, Rogen; base rates 0.53 / 0.51 / 0.36); 'fit' 11/20, 11/20, 10/20 = chance (within-person AUC
   0.60 / 0.40 / 0.40). Judge top 20: heavier 17 / 14 / 15, fit 18 / 20 / 19. s20 0.144-0.183 -> rule says judge

8. Face-filter purity, seen on the way: 4/208 sampled red boxes were a dog's face, all from one 'person'
   (47642109@N04#2): the buffalo_l detector + expansion made a person group out of a pet. Not this question, but it is
   a wrong-photo source for "photos of me" that nobody has measured.

LIMITS
- People sets here are 62-282 photos (median 98); Reza's face set is 41,541. In a set that large, s20 will run higher
  and most conditions will have >= 20 real matches, so the base-rate part of finding 4 shrinks. What does NOT depend on
  set size: (a) the score scale inside a person set is lower than the whole-library scale the threshold was fitted on,
  (b) s20 does not certify 'enough' at any threshold here, (c) the vector cannot rank expressions/states (smiling,
  tired, fit) - within-person AUC 0.40-0.68. A large-set test needs one public person with >= 2,000 natural photos;
  none exists in what we hold (IMDB-WIKI crops are face-only).
- Judge-relative numbers inherit the judge's errors (tired: 0/16 by eye).
- Eye sample: 26 photos per condition, 10 per stratum: intervals are wide (e.g. 9/10 = 0.55-1.00).

RECOMMENDATION: make person+look eligible for vector-first: NO.
- Keep vectorFirstIneligible's "person search" gate. Measured, not assumed: 0/256 person searches reach 0.2074, and no
  lower threshold is safe (best bin 9/20 enough; refit finds no T in 17/17 folds).
- Keep likelyAtOnce off for person searches too (0.77 judge-rel. overall, 0.33 for 'tired', 0.52 for 'eating').
- What IS usable from this: inside the face-filtered set, judge in VECTOR ORDER for scene/object looks (within-person
  AUC 0.83-0.99): beach / outdoors / snow / hat / sunglasses / eating matches sit at the top of the vector ranking, so
  the judge's first screen fills in far fewer calls than judging all 41.5k. That is the speed lever for person+look,
  not skipping the judge. For expressions/states (smiling, tired, heavier/fit) the vector order barely helps.
- "Photos where I look tired" needs its own answer: neither the vector nor the 4B judge finds tired photos by eye here.
