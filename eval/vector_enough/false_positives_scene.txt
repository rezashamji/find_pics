  strictness sits in 'unsure', which RESULTS 34/45 count as not a match: 15 of the 45 unsure judge-yes photos (13 more borderline) are
  matches by the written protocol or by what a user means (a puppy ON a beach, a cake being cut, a sun at the horizon).
  Specific label/protocol problems: (a) beach: the protocol says 'sand/shingle shore ... at sea or lake' but labellers
  marked shingle lakeshores unsure/no (FPbe20 2924571902, FPbe21/22 borderline); (b) beach: photos taken ON a beach
  (wet sand floor, waterline, loungers on sand, night beach) were unsure because the 'scene' is not shown; (c) sunset:
  the protocol's 'sun low/at horizon' clause was not applied when colours were muted (FPsu91 8168098427, FPsu94
  3094805889), and sunset afterglow skies filling the frame were unsure (FPsu87 7094109721, FPsu88 6886753591, FPsu86
  4937491102); (d) food: 'food is a main element' was not applied when people were also in the frame (cake being cut,
  heaped cookie plate, paella in the foreground) or the fruit looked waxy (FPfo73 10033495705).
  So 0.57/0.66/0.72 understate precision somewhat, but most of the gap is real judge error, not label strictness.
